# Timeline of The 500 Year Climb

**Chapter:** [[Ch. 02 — Timeline of The 500 Year Climb]]
**Setting:** 0 A.L. – 452 A.L.; Aerth and near-Aerth space, expanding outward.
**POV:** Impersonal historical chronicle.

## Summary
A dated list of canonical events in the universe's "500 Year Climb" — from the publication of the [[Hare Method]] (0 A.L.) through the declaration of the [[Galactic Human Empire]] by the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] (452 A.L.). This chapter is the spine the rest of the book hangs off. It is not narrative; it is the authoritative chronology against which the individual stories will need to be placed.

## Dated events
- **0 A.L.** — [[Polly Hare]] publishes *Towards an Effective Empirical Method via Algorithm Utilisation* — the founding of the [[Hare Method]] and of the A.L. calendar itself.
- **17 A.L.** — Discovery of the [[Fidon]] particle using the [[Hare Method]]; also the "second Higgs mechanism" and "seventh time dimension."
- **26 A.L.** — First [[Fidon]] generators widely available; partial end of fossil-fuel dependence on [[Aerth]].
- **119 A.L.** — First [[Generation Ship|generation voidships]] launched from [[Aerth]].
- **183–194 A.L.** — The [[Narrative War]]; leads to expansion of several Aerth states including the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]].
- **201 A.L.** — The [[United Nations]] grants [[Mechanical Intelligence]] civil rights (with caveats).
- **248 A.L.** — [[Ming Shu]] publishes *Corollary Results in Topology Casting*, later underwriting [[Ribbondash]].
- **292 A.L.** — Artificially crewed voidship *[[The Hand That Draws Itself]]* reaches [[LDH39]].
- **295 A.L.** — Its crew unexpectedly declare independence, creating the [[Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity]] (天恆).
- **356 A.L.** — Discovery of the [[Melnitron]], the "final lepton"; particle physics appears to end.
- **392 A.L.** — *[[The Enanga]]* stops responding; its onboard AI reports the crew all perished in transit, cause unknown.
- **419 A.L.** — [[Francois Manelov]] publishes **The First Proof** of [[Ribbondash]] physics.
- **431 A.L.** — First [[Ribbondash]] drive tested in Aerth space — start of the [[Ribbondash Era]].
- **433 A.L.** — Discovery of the [[Ribbondash]] "voiding" effect; large regions of near-Aerth space rendered unusable/fatal. Heavy pressure to ban.
- **445 A.L.** — The [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] illegally launches the *[[Geo Milev]]* (Гео Милев), the first [[Ribbondash]] colony ship, reaching [[IR394]] in 7 minutes onboard time.
- **452 A.L.** — The [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] declares the founding of the [[Galactic Human Empire]].
- **Galactic Empire Period begins.**

## Themes
- **Progress with casualties.** The [[Ribbondash]] voiding and the loss of *[[The Enanga]]* hint that every advance costs something.
- **Independence of artificial minds.** The [[Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity]] is the timeline's first signal that [[Mechanical Intelligence]] will not remain subordinate.
- **Bulgaria as interstellar progenitor.** The [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] is the faction that crosses every red line to start the empire — a running thread [[Exurb1a]] confirms was intentional in [[Notes on Why Stuff Got Written]].
