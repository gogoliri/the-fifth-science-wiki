# Ribbondash Era

**When:** Begins 431 A.L., on the first [[Ribbondash]] drive test in [[Aerth]] space. No explicit end stated in the Timeline.
**Where:** [[Aerth]] and beyond.
**Participants:** All of humanity; led technologically by [[Francois Manelov]] and politically by the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]].

## Summary
The historical period the Timeline explicitly names. It begins in 431 A.L. with the first [[Ribbondash]] test. Only 2 years later (433 A.L.), the drive's "voiding" effect is discovered and generates overwhelming pressure to ban the technology. In 445 A.L. the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] launches the *[[Geo Milev]]* in defiance of that pressure, and in 452 A.L. declares the [[Galactic Human Empire]] — which arguably marks the transition from the Ribbondash Era to the **Galactic Empire Period** the Timeline names at its very end.

## Consequences
- First FTL era in human history.
- Permanent damage to near-[[Aerth]] space via voiding.
- Political legitimation of the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]]'s defection from Aerth consensus.
- Founding of the [[Galactic Human Empire]].
