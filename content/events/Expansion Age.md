# Expansion Age

**Type:** Era of rapid interstellar colonisation by humanity from [[Aerth]].
**When:** Beginning around the era of [[Mitchley Chang]]'s voidship arrival scene — plausibly **~619 A.L.**, roughly 500 years after the first generation-ship launches of **~119 A.L.** ([[Jenny Dunne]]'s scene).

## Summary
The period during which humanity transitions from slow generation-ship pioneering to routine arrival at alien worlds. [[Mitchley Chang]] stands at a porthole watching a new world come into view, and this single image is the book's shorthand for the Expansion Age mood: colonists routinely reach destinations that an earlier generation had to be frozen for centuries to approach.

In Ch. 03 the Expansion Age is used as a dating pin: it comes *after* [[Jenny Dunne]] (the first generation-voidship launch) and *long before* [[K. Pasternak]] (the high empire), bracketing the middle of the wanderer's millennia-long walk through history.
