# Vasily Incident

**When:** Unstated A.L. year. Late empire, some years before [[The Ertian Surgeon]]'s final topolog trip to [[Aerth]].
**Where:** Orbit of [[Orb Ertia]]'s [[TZ Star|Thorne–Żytkow motherstar]].
**Participants:** [[Vasily]] (destroyed), its ~120,000 longsleepers (killed), [[Ivan Tellinger]] (bifurcated but still alive), [[Tabitha Dimitrova]] and the Empiral Special Incidents Team, [[The Ertian Surgeon]] and a boarding party of medical personnel from Ertian hospitals, and **an unknown perfectly-spherical artifact** that exited the motherstar at 70% c and passed straight through the ship.

## Summary
The third recorded TZ-exit artifact event in the [[Galactic Human Empire]], and the first the empire is able to extract material evidence from. Three spheres across several centuries have exited Thorne–Żytkow stars at 70% c — the first scanned from afar by a science voidship, the second tracked by an astronomy satellite at [[Orb Flain]] (confirming composition and a ~90° mid-flight course change), and this third one striking the passing hollowship *[[Vasily]]* during its slingshot transit through the [[Orb Ertia|Ertia]] system.

The *Vasily*'s presence was not accidental. As [[The Ertian Surgeon]] extracts from [[Tabitha Dimitrova]] in the Shienae operating room, the empire has been **deliberately routing hollowships through TZ-star systems** in the hope of another exit event. The *Vasily*'s 120,000 longsleepers were the acceptable cost of the observation.

### Timeline as the surgeon experienced it
1. The *Vasily* enters the Ertian system to slingshot off the TZ motherstar.
2. Several days of ground-side boredom on Ertia.
3. A wall-image shows the hollowship tearing open: "gas spilling out, bodies visible."
4. Aid workers press-gang Ertian hospital medics into flyers and boost them out of atmosphere.
5. Dimitrova briefs the medics in zero-G, fits them into voidsuits, and leads a boarding party through an emergency airlock.
6. The surgeon willtech-propels himself up the winding entry tunnel past floating body parts into the great longsleep cavern, then to the bridge, then to Lab 9 Phi, where he finds the bifurcated-but-conscious [[Ivan Tellinger]].
7. Tellinger is brought back to Shienae hospital on Ertia.
8. Dimitrova threatens the surgeon with a scalpel; then breaks and explains the three-sphere pattern and the empire's "experiment"; then threatens [[Eda Hamebe]] by name and storms out.
9. Tellinger is shipped onward to Aerth "if he lives... if he doesn't live, same applies."

### Consequences
- The empire recovers **silver holographic nanoparticle residue** from Tellinger's wound — the cauterisation material that turns out to be the encoding substrate for [[The Dictionary]].
- The story of the experiment leaks years later out of the "science division on [[New Rosance|Rosance]]" — the same leak that decodes the 3D-binary dictionary and makes it empire-public.
- [[Eda Hamebe]] judges that the decoded dictionary "is going to change things, really change things. Soon."
