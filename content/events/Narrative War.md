# Narrative War

**When:** 183–194 A.L. (11 years).
**Where:** [[Aerth]].
**Participants:** Unstated. The [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] is one of several Aerth states that **expand** as a result of the war's outcome.

## Summary
An eleven-year Aerth-side conflict. Ends in 194 A.L., after which several existing Aerth states — including the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] — expand. The war's evocative name ("Narrative") is unexplained in the Timeline; it may refer to information warfare, memetic conflict, or a dispute over historical interpretation, but nothing in the Timeline pins down the meaning.

The war's most important downstream consequence is the post-war strengthening of the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]], which is the state that will later launch *[[Geo Milev]]* and found the [[Galactic Human Empire]]. Without the 194 A.L. expansion, the 445 A.L. launch arguably doesn't have a polity big enough to pull it off.

## Consequences
- Post-war expansion of several Aerth states.
- [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] emerges larger — arguably the seed of its later galactic role.
- 7 years later, the [[United Nations]] grants [[Mechanical Intelligence]] civil rights (201 A.L.) — chronologically close enough that it's worth asking whether the rights grant is a post-war peace dividend.
