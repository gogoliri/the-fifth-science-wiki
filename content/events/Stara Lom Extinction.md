# Stara Lom Extinction

**When:** Complete by **12,847 A.L.**, when all communications from the planet stop.
**Where:** [[Stara Lom]]
**Participants:** [[Shervance Ek]], [[Maximilian]], a cyclist, the mayor, and then the general public.

## Summary
The spread of the [[Cognitotronic Bombardment Device|Want Machine]] across Stara Lom. After stripping themselves of all desire, Ek and Maximilian are left with one surviving desire, Maximilian's wish to help others. Each new subject is stripped and implanted with the desire to bring in more ("the Good Work"). Everyone on the planet passes through the machine and then, with no inclination left even to eat, dies. The whole population of seven million is found dead, except for a cat and a chicken. "No sign of struggle was detected in a single instance. Nor dissatisfaction either."

## Consequences
- The planet goes silent. The narrator's account is reconstructed from forensic reports and **illegal** visits by "ribbondashing youths", implying a quarantine.
- Stara Lom stops being "a shining jewel in the empire's crown."
