# Mandala

**Type:** Language of the [[Galactic Human Empire]] in the ~2641 A.L. era.

## Summary
The working language of [[K. Pasternak]]'s court. Named once in passing — [[The First Wanderer]] notes that Pasternak "speaks Mandala" — and treated as the default in the high-empire setting. The name suggests a fusion language; beyond that the story gives no linguistic detail.

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 03 — For Every Dove a Bullet]]: Named as the language of [[K. Pasternak]].

- [[K. Pasternak]], [[Galactic Human Empire]]

- Is Mandala a lingua franca or one of several empire languages? Unstated.
