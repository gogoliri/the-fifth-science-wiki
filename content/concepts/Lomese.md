# Lomese

## Summary
The language of [[Stara Lom]]: "the only language in the galaxy with seventeen versions of thank you and no imperatives." Gratitude is easy to express and commands are nearly impossible, so requests are phrased gently ("Perhaps you could take my bags up to the room?"), even, comically, threats ("Now it is time for you to fucking die, I'm afraid"). The narrator credits the language with teaching the Lomese to *temper their desires*. Slavery is unthinkable, anger is rare and waiters are treated very well. That makes the planet an ironic setting for the [[Cognitotronic Bombardment Device|Want Machine]], which tempers desire all the way to zero.
