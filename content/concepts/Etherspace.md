# Etherspace

## Summary
Etherspace is the medium a ship enters when it jumps to [[Ribbondash]] to cross interstellar distances. It is hostile to complexity: computers stop working and awake humans "break", so complex machinery is switched off and passengers are put into [[Transitsleep|transitsleep]], leaving only very simple life support and a wire-and-pulley control yoke. *"There is no time and no duration in etherspace. All events occur at once."* Only [[Lantern|lanterns]] can stay awake there, and from inside it they see past and future and learn [[True Names|true names]]. From outside, a ship entering it vanishes completely ("space lensed for a moment, then the ship was gone"). The narrator calls it *"everwhen ... that place between places. Up to a boundary and beyond it."*

## In the book
- [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]]: The lantern explains etherspace travel and watches a voidskipper enter it.

## Real-world grounding
- Classic SF hyperspace that is hostile to minds (Miéville's *Embassytown* "immer", which the author cites; Warhammer 40k's warp).
- Timelessness echoes block-universe physics (see [[Radetsky's Law]]).
