# Packet

**Type:** The wanderer's term for an ordinary embodied human consciousness — a "packet" of mind bound to a single brain and lifespan.

## Summary
The word [[The First Wanderer]] (and, later, [[Evie]]) uses for every mind that isn't a [[Wanderer]]. A packet is:

- **Bounded**: it lives inside exactly one brain and dies with it.
- **Opaque from outside**: a wanderer entering a packet experiences its "surface" longings first, then its self-image, then — beneath both — its real will.
- **Layered**: the wanderer describes at least three strata (conscious aims, self-regard, deep drive) in every packet he inspects.
- **Nameable**: the wanderer adopts his packets' names as his own. His first packet [[Winston Earnest|Earnest]] gives him the name *Earnest*, which [[Evie]] throws back at him as the execution line.

Packets are also the wanderer's moral blind spot. He reasons that because political systems "are self-correcting," the evil his packet [[K. Pasternak]] does as Marquis is not his problem. [[Evie]] disagrees — this is exactly the disagreement that kills them both.
