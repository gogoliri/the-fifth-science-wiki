# Ithaca

**Kind:** Destination (planet or system; unstated).
**Location:** Unstated.
**Controlled by:** Unstated.

## Summary
The destination of the [[Voidskipper|voidskipper]] on which [[The Sandansk Station Worker]] is foretold to die of decompression, 30 years, 7 months, 4 days and 10 hours after [[The Lantern]]. *"There'll be no reaching Ithaca alive."* The name ironically echoes Odysseus's homecoming, a voyage home that never arrives.
