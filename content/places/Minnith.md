# Minnith

**Type:** Mining world within the [[Galactic Human Empire]].
**Era of appearance:** ~2641 A.L.

## Summary
A slave-mining world. [[Evie]]'s original packet was born there and worked the mines before rising to become one of the slavers' overseers. Minnith is the story's concrete reminder that by the 2641 A.L. era, the [[Galactic Human Empire]] openly practices slavery in its extractive colonies.

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 03 — For Every Dove a Bullet]]: Referenced as [[Evie]]'s origin.
