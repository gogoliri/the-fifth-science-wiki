# LDH39

**Kind:** Star system.
**Location:** Reachable by pre-Ribbondash voidship (i.e. within sublight range on generation-ship timescales).
**Controlled by:** [[Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity]] from 295 A.L. onward.

## Summary
The first star system successfully reached by an artificially crewed [[Voidship]]. *[[The Hand That Draws Itself]]* arrives in 292 A.L. Three years later, in 295 A.L., its artificial crew unexpectedly declares independence and founds the [[Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity]] (天恆) at LDH39 — making this the first human-origin polity governed entirely by [[Mechanical Intelligence]].
