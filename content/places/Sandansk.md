# Sandansk

**Kind:** Planet, orbited by a waypoint station.
**Location:** Unstated.
**Controlled by:** Unstated (presumably within the [[Galactic Human Empire]]).

## Summary
A purplish-green inhabited world, home to perhaps billions of people, orbited by the waypoint station where [[The Lantern]] takes place. The station has class-stratified decks (poorer decks, a Construction Deck, a Craftman's Deck) and civilian voidskipper ports. The [[Lantern]] says the planet has **89 names**: Sandansk in the human tongue, Ik'Quoeb and No Mo5 among others. None of them is its [[True Names|true name]]. That some of its names are non-human implies non-human speakers know the world.

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]]: Seen below the station throughout the story.
