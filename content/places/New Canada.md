# New Canada

**Kind:** Ice world.
**Location:** Unstated.
**Controlled by:** [[Galactic Human Empire]] (implied).

## Summary
[[Shervance Ek]]'s home world, home of the New Canadian University where Ek and [[Nadastra]] work together as young researchers. Its culture apparently prizes modesty: Ek "believed gratitude was second only to modesty."
