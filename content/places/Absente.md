# Absente

**Kind:** Destination (planet or system; unstated).
**Location:** Unstated.
**Controlled by:** Unstated.

## Summary
The destination of the [[Lantern]]'s [[Voidskipper|voidskipper]], leaving the [[Sandansk]] waypoint station "at ten tomorrow, Standard Time". The lantern invites [[The Sandansk Station Worker]] to ride up front with it on this trip for his first sight of [[Etherspace]], before going on to [[Aerth]] for training. It is the escape from the [[Ithaca]] fate.
