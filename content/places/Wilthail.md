# Wilthail

**Type:** Small village on [[Aerth]] ("or some such").
**Era of appearance:** Plausibly **~119 A.L.**

## Summary
Home of [[Jenny Dunne]], who travelled from here to watch the first generation-voidship launch. Named vaguely — the wanderer remembers the village only as "Wilthail, or some such," which may mean the place-name is half-forgotten even inside his memory, or that it is a real obscure A.L.-era village whose name the narrator never bothered to verify.
