# Winchester

**Kind:** Settlement (village or small town)
**Location:** [[Aerth]]
**Controlled by:** [[Galactic Human Empire]]

## Summary
A settlement on [[Aerth]] where [[Eda Hamebe]] and her family have chosen to live in deliberate withdrawal from late-empire life. Eda and her husband are both implant-free (described as rare on late-empire Aerth), living in an ancient stone house on a farm. Her husband's first language is Anglish — possibly suggesting Winchester is in a region where Anglish is the dominant vernacular. The name echoes the real-world English city.

When [[The Ertian Surgeon]] arrives on Aerth via topolog for the last time, Winchester is where Eda takes him after dinner to see [[Henrietta]]'s grave. It is also where he spent his final night before death.
