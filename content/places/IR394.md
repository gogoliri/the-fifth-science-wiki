# IR394

**Kind:** Star system.
**Location:** Distant enough from [[Aerth]] to be impossible via sublight; only the [[Ribbondash]] drive makes it reachable.
**Controlled by:** [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] (445 A.L.); presumably the seed system of the [[Galactic Human Empire]] (452 A.L.).

## Summary
The destination of the first-ever [[Ribbondash]] colony ship, the *[[Geo Milev]]*, launched illegally by the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] in 445 A.L. The voyage takes only **7 minutes onboard time**, which is the timeline's first explicit demonstration that Ribbondash is a real FTL drive rather than a theoretical one. Seven years later, the Bulgaric Republic declares the founding of the [[Galactic Human Empire]] — and IR394, as the first extra-solar Ribbondash destination, is the logical seed for that empire.
