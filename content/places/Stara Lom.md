# Stara Lom

**Kind:** Medium-size forest world.
**Location:** Unstated, within the [[Galactic Human Empire]].
**Controlled by:** [[Galactic Human Empire]].

## Summary
A mild, beautiful forest world with blueish forests and delicious food. Its inhabitants just call it "The World." It is a favourite empire holiday destination, especially for physicists, mostly because of the gentle locals and their language, [[Lomese]], which has seventeen ways to say thank you and no imperatives. Slavery is unthinkable, anger rare, and waiters very well treated. Visas are easy if you're polite. [[Shervance Ek]] builds the [[Cognitotronic Bombardment Device|Want Machine]] in a house on the equator, and by **12,847 A.L.** all seven million inhabitants are dead without struggle (the [[Stara Lom Extinction]]). Only a cat and a chicken survive. Before this it was "a shining jewel in the empire's crown."
