# Orb Flain

**Kind:** Colony world (or station-bearing system)
**Location:** Unstated — within empire space
**Controlled by:** [[Galactic Human Empire]]

## Summary
The system where an astronomy satellite tracked the second known TZ-exit artifact event, approximately sixty years after the first. The object — perfectly spherical, compositionally identical to the first — changed course by roughly 90° without decelerating ("possibly with a little Higgs trick, we don't know"), giving the empire its first close-range scan of an artifact exit. This is the event that established the artifacts as genuinely anomalous rather than natural ejecta. Orb Flain is not visited as a setting; it appears only as a reference point in the Vasily Incident's backstory.

## Appearances
