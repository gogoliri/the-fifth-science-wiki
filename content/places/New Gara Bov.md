# New Gara Bov

**Kind:** Colony world
**Location:** Unstated — within empire space
**Controlled by:** [[Galactic Human Empire]]

## Summary
A colony world within the empire that briefly rendezvoused with a moon-sized entity: a mass composed entirely of nerve filaments with no method of sensing the outside world. The creature orbited New Gara Bov for several days before departing. Several scholars hypothesise that a mind of the creature's scale could compute the universe from first principles — deducing its location and environment by extrapolating from base logic alone, without any sensory input whatsoever. The narrator notes that "Polly Hare's devotees still remain strong in some regions of the empire" in relation to this hypothesis, linking the creature to the book's panpsychist tradition.
