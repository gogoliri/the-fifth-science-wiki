# Aerth

**Kind:** Planet (the human homeworld).
**Location:** Sol system (implied; not explicitly named).
**Controlled by:** Multiple Aerth states in the pre-Empire period, including the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] and, at least at the [[United Nations]] level, a planetary body.

## Summary
The human homeworld. "Aerth" is the consistent in-universe spelling and is the origin point of the 500 Year Climb: the publication of the [[Hare Method]] (0 A.L.), the discovery of the [[Fidon]] (17 A.L.), the launch of the first [[Voidship|voidships]] (119 A.L.), and the declaration of the [[Galactic Human Empire]] (452 A.L.) all happen here or around it. After the Ribbondash "voiding" incident of 433 A.L., large regions of **near-Aerth space** are rendered unusable and fatal to pass through — implying the planet's orbital environment is materially damaged by its own children's technology.
