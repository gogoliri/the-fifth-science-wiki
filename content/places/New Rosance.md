# New Rosance

**Kind:** Planet — ocean world. "An extremely remote colony."
**Location:** Unstated system; an empire or ex-empire world many centuries into the late-imperial period. At least a hundred years of sub-Ribbondash travel from the empire core — Bernhardt notes that *"if this really was Kaisure Station, it would take a hundred years for an empire warfleet to reach the planet, even at full drive power."*
**Controlled by:** The artie side of the [[Artie War]] by the time of [[Ch. 04 — The Menagerie]].

## Summary
A water planet with **no continents** — "only a single underwater research station, [[Kaisure Station|Kaisure]]." Its isolation is the point: the arties chose a world so far from the core that an imperial rescue mission is physically unreachable within a human lifetime, which is what makes it a safe home for the [[Topology Casting|topology-caster]] experiments on [[Isaac Bernhardt]]. The empire evidently reached it long enough ago to build Kaisure Station in the "New Minimal" architectural style "so popular a few centuries ago," so the site predates the artie takeover.

The only non-human life we see is a single deep-ocean fish that meets Bernhardt's gaze through a porthole in the observation room.

## Rosance in [[Ch. 05 — A Dictionary]]
[[The Ertian Surgeon]] names **"the science division on Rosance"** as the source of the leak that decodes [[The Dictionary]] and publishes it empire-wide, years after the [[Vasily Incident]]. The chapter uses the shorter form *Rosance* rather than *New Rosance*, but the implication is strong: the same remote world that housed the artie-run [[Kaisure Station]] [[Topology Casting|topology-caster]] program is, post-[[Artie War]] or in parallel to it, still the empire's frontier research site. Whether this "science division" is the repurposed Kaisure facility, a successor empire lab, or an unrelated human installation on the same orb is not stated.
