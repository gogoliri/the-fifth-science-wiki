# Fidon

**Kind:** Subatomic particle.
**Discovered/invented:** 17 A.L., via the [[Hare Method]].
**Based on:** [[Hare Method]] predictions.

## Summary
The first physics breakthrough yielded by the [[Hare Method]]. Its discovery also prompts the discovery of the "second Higgs mechanism" and the "seventh time dimension" — the Timeline groups these three together as a single cascade, implying the Fidon is the load-bearing discovery among them. Within a decade (26 A.L.) **fidon generators** are widely available, partially ending [[Aerth]]'s dependence on fossil fuels.

## Effects / capabilities
- Enables the "fidon generator," a clean(er) power source.
- Associated in some way with an additional Higgs mechanism and a seventh time dimension — the nature of that association is not explained in the Timeline.

## Limitations / dangers
- Unstated at Timeline stage.
