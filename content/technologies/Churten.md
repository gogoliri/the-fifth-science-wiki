# Churten

**Kind:** Unit of beam energy used in [[Galactic Human Empire|Empire]]-era particle physics.
**Era of use:** ~2641 A.L. and surrounding period.

## Summary
The churten is the energy unit in which [[K. Pasternak]]'s collider is specified and primed. The story does not give a conversion to 21st-century units; churtens are treated as the local standard in the high-empire era, the same way "GeV" is treated as obvious in our own physics.

Only two churten values are named on-page:

- **5 churtens** — the energy at which [[Nootics]] theory predicts the [[Nooticle]] should appear. This is the design target of the Aerth-Luna-diameter collider.
- **4.3 churtens** — the priming level the collider actually reaches before [[Evie]] seals the beam chamber. The 5-churten experiment is never run.
