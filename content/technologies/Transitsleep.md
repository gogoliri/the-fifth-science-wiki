# Transitsleep

**Kind:** Suspended-animation process for interstellar passengers.
**Discovered/invented:** Unstated.
**Based on:** Unstated.

## Summary
The standard empire practice of putting passengers into suspended sleep for a journey. [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]] gives the reason: during [[Ribbondash]] crossings of [[Etherspace]], awake humans "do not function", so they are put into transitsleep, "packed away like sardines", or they come out broken. The narrator also calls it **longsleep** ("the crew all frosted in longsleep"), the same term [[Ch. 05 — A Dictionary]] uses for the ~120,000 sleepers of the *[[Vasily]]*. This page assumes the two terms name the same technology.

## Effects / capabilities
- Lets thousands of passengers survive etherspace crossings and long hauls with only a skeleton crew (or, on a voidskipper, only a [[Lantern]]) awake.

## Limitations / dangers
- Sleepers cannot respond to emergencies: the *Vasily*'s longsleepers all die in their chambers during the [[Vasily Incident]].

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 05 — A Dictionary]]: Longsleep chambers by the thousand line the *[[Vasily]]*'s interior.
- [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]]: The reason it exists. Etherspace breaks awake humans.
