# Voidskipper

**Kind:** Civilian [[Ribbondash]]-capable passenger ship; a class of [[Voidship]].
**Discovered/invented:** Unstated; routine by the time of [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]].
**Based on:** [[Ribbondash]] drive; piloted through [[Etherspace]] by a [[Lantern]].

## Summary
The everyday interstellar passenger ship of [[The Lantern]]. It docks at civilian ports on the [[Sandansk]] waypoint station and looks like "a nimble black fish". A small transparent bubble protrudes from the front, where a lantern rides. The passengers and crew ride "frosted in longsleep" while ship drones clean the corridors, canteens and laboratories. Departure sequence as seen from the station: back away from the dock, swing the nose towards the destination, power up the main drive, activate ribbondash, and space lenses for a moment as the ship disappears.

## Effects / capabilities
- Routine interstellar transit (e.g. to [[Absente]] and [[Ithaca]]).

## Limitations / dangers
- Can be fatal: [[The Sandansk Station Worker|the narrator]] is foretold to die in a decompression accident aboard one.
- Depends on a lantern and on almost all systems being switched off during the jump.

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]]: Several seen from the bar window. The lantern's own leaves "at ten tomorrow, Standard Time".
