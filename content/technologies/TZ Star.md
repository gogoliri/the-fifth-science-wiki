# TZ Star

**Kind:** A Thorne–Żytkow object — a stellar body with a neutron star core. In-universe the class is infrastructure as much as astrophysics: hollowships use them for slingshot acceleration, and unexplained artifacts keep exiting them at relativistic speed.
**Discovered/invented:** Natural object; the type is real (Thorne–Żytkow objects are a theoretical class named for the two astrophysicists who proposed them). The empire's operational *use* of them for [[Hollowship]] slingshot is the invented part.
**Based on:** A neutron star embedded inside a normal stellar envelope.

## Summary
[[The Ertian Surgeon|The surgeon]] explains TZ stars for the Empiral Archive as a class "known across the empire for its miraculous flashes and fits, given its neutron star core." Hollowships routinely enter TZ-star systems to accelerate off the motherstar, a manoeuvre that saves them "hundreds of years in journey duration."

The chapter's reveal is that TZ stars are also **the exit points of an unknown phenomenon**. Three recorded instances across several centuries of empire observation:

1. A "science voidship" detects an object exiting an unnamed TZ star at **~70% c**, in composition unlike any known empire material. The voidship cannot catch it.
2. About sixty years later, an astronomy satellite at [[Orb Flain]] catches a second exit event. The object is **perfectly spherical**, changes course by ~90° without pausing ("possibly with a little Higgs trick, we don't know"), and is compositionally identical to the first.
3. The [[Vasily Incident]] at [[Orb Ertia]] — a third sphere, same composition, same 70% c — hits the hollowship *Vasily* and is the first one the empire is able to examine (via the cauterising nanoparticle residue on [[Ivan Tellinger]]).

Because all three events come out of TZ stars, the [[Galactic Human Empire]] has been stationing hollowships in TZ-star systems on **purpose**, using ordinary slingshot traffic as cover for what is effectively an empire-funded observation campaign. The *Vasily*'s 120,000 longsleepers died for the data.

## Effects / capabilities
- **Slingshot acceleration** for [[Hollowship|hollowships]] — the operational reason the empire routes long-haul traffic through TZ systems.
- **Exit points for unknown spheres** — a repeating anomaly the empire treats as actionable intelligence.

## Limitations / dangers
- TZ stars are dangerous neighbours under any ordinary model (neutron-star cores, flashes, fits).
- The sphere events make TZ systems physically deadly to any ship passing through at the wrong moment.

## Open questions
- **Why TZ stars specifically?** No in-story answer. - **How many total exit events has the empire catalogued?** Three are named; the pattern of deliberate hollowship placement suggests the empire expects more.
