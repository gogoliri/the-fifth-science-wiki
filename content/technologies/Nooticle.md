# Nooticle

**Type:** Hypothetical fundamental particle (or field quantum) of consciousness — the proposed fifth-force carrier that [[Nootics]] was designed to detect.
**Era:** Theorised by ~2641 A.L.; undetected as of Ch. 03's end.

## Summary
The nooticle is the concrete physical object that, if it exists, would make [[Mentalic Ontology]] a science rather than a philosophy. The theory: every physical interaction known to the [[Galactic Human Empire]]'s physicists involves one of four force-carriers; the nooticle would be the **fifth**, coupling specifically to the pattern-structure of mind. Finding it would collapse consciousness, [[Panpsychism]], and [[Nootics]] into a single verified branch of physics — the book's title concept of **[[The Fifth Science]]**.

The predicted detection regime is **5 [[Churten|churtens]]** of beam energy — more than any existing accelerator can reach. [[K. Pasternak]], under [[The First Wanderer]]'s guidance, orders construction of a collider physically larger than the Aerth-Luna diameter to hit this regime. The collider is primed to 4.3 churtens when [[Evie]] shuts it down; the nooticle is never observed on-page.
