# Ribbondash

**Kind:** Faster-than-light drive technology.
**Discovered/invented:** First mathematical proof 419 A.L. ([[Francois Manelov]]); first physical test 431 A.L.
**Based on:** [[Topology Casting]], originally from [[Ming Shu]]'s 248 A.L. paper.

## Summary
The [[Galactic Human Empire]] is only possible because of Ribbondash. The theoretical groundwork is laid in 248 A.L. by [[Ming Shu]], but sits idle for 171 years until [[Francois Manelov]] publishes **The First Proof** in 419 A.L. The first test in 431 A.L. marks the start of the [[Ribbondash Era]]. Ribbondash travel is extraordinarily fast — the *[[Geo Milev]]* reaches [[IR394]] in **7 minutes onboard time**.

## Effects / capabilities
- FTL transit in minutes where sublight travel would take generations.

## Limitations / dangers
- **The voiding effect** (discovered 433 A.L., two years after the first test): large regions of near-[[Aerth]] space are rendered unusable and fatal to pass through. This causes overwhelming pressure to ban the technology — pressure the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] defies in 445 A.L. by launching the *[[Geo Milev]]*.
