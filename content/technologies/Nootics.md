# Nootics

**Type:** The [[Galactic Human Empire]]'s science of [[Consciousness]]. The in-universe discipline studying the physical basis of mind.
**Era:** Established by at least ~2641 A.L.; a recognised academic field by the time of [[K. Pasternak]].

## Summary
Nootics is the direct descendant of [[Henry Berkhamsted]]'s [[Mentalic Ontology]]: the idea that mind is a pattern of information which should, in principle, leave a physical trace detectable by instruments. A practitioner is a **nootician**; [[K. Pasternak]] is introduced as one of the most respected nooticians in the Empire.

The core research goal in Pasternak's era is to find the **[[Nooticle]]** — the hypothetical fundamental particle (or force-carrier) of consciousness, the missing fifth alongside the four fundamental interactions of physics. The Pasternak-narrator collider is designed to collide beams at **5 [[Churten|churtens]]** because that is the energy regime at which the nooticle is predicted to appear.

Nootics is a serious, funded science — but not yet validated. The collider is primed to 4.3 churtens when [[Evie]] seals the chamber; the experiment is never run.
