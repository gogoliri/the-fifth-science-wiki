# Voidship

**Kind:** Interstellar spacecraft category.
**Discovered/invented:** First launched 119 A.L. from [[Aerth]] as "generation voidships."
**Based on:** Post-[[Fidon]] power technology (implied).

## Summary
The Timeline's catch-all term for sublight interstellar vessels, divided into at least two subtypes: **[[Generation Ship|generation voidships]]** (biologically crewed, transit over lifetimes) and **artificially crewed voidships** (machine minds, indifferent to travel time). The first generation voidships launch in 119 A.L.; *[[The Hand That Draws Itself]]* is the first artificially crewed voidship to successfully reach another star in 292 A.L.; *[[The Enanga]]*'s loss in 392 A.L. suggests the biologically-crewed approach is fragile; and in 431 A.L. voidships are superseded by the first [[Ribbondash]] drive.

[[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]] shows that "voidship" stays in use as the general term for empire-era starships *after* Ribbondash. Voidships "reach the stars" by entering [[Etherspace]] on a [[Ribbondash]] jump, with passengers in [[Transitsleep|transitsleep]], complex machinery switched off, and a [[Lantern]] piloting from a nose bubble by wire-and-pulley controls. The [[Voidskipper]] is a civilian subclass.

## Effects / capabilities
- Sublight interstellar travel (early generation/AI-crewed voidships).
- [[Ribbondash]] crossings through [[Etherspace]] (empire-era voidships; Ch. 08).

## Limitations / dangers
- Transit times long enough that biological crews may not survive ([[The Enanga]]).
- In etherspace, computers and awake humans fail. Only very simple life support and wire-linked control fins stay online (Ch. 08).
