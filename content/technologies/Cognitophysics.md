# Cognitophysics

**Kind:** Scientific discipline.
**Discovered/invented:** Established by the era of [[Ch. 09 — The Want Machine]] (before 12,847 A.L.).
**Based on:** The [[Cognitron]].

## Summary
"That branch of science concerned with the mechanisms of consciousness." In Ek's time it is a mature field: it has found the [[Cognitron]], but until [[Shervance Ek]] it cannot *manipulate* consciousness. Practitioners include Ek and, four hundred years later, [[Amelia Minsk]] (seven types of love). It is the later counterpart of [[Nootics]], Ch. 03's ~2641 A.L. science of consciousness, which was still hunting its particle.
