# Cognitron

**Kind:** Subatomic particle.
**Discovered/invented:** Before the events of [[Ch. 09 — The Want Machine]] (before 12,847 A.L.). Discoverer unstated.
**Based on:** [[Cognitophysics]].

## Summary
*"By then the cognitron had been discovered of course, the subatomic particle responsible for producing self-awareness in all things."* This is the strongest in-universe confirmation so far that consciousness has a particle basis and is present "in all things", which is [[Panpsychism]] and [[The Fifth Science]] as settled physics. Knowing the particle doesn't immediately give control ("a man might name a species of bird but it doesn't allow him to fly"). [[Shervance Ek]]'s [[Cognitotronic Bombardment Device]] is the first technology to manipulate consciousness through it.

## Effects / capabilities
- Produces self-awareness. Bombarding the brain with it at high amplitude (presumably) allows desire-editing.
