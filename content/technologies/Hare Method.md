# Hare Method

**Kind:** Empirical research method — "an effective empirical method via algorithm utilisation."
**Discovered/invented:** 0 A.L. by [[Polly Hare]].
**Based on:** Algorithmic exploration of physical theory space (exact mechanism not described).

## Summary
Published in 0 A.L. as *Towards an Effective Empirical Method via Algorithm Utilisation*. The publication of this paper **defines the A.L. calendar** (Anno Logicae), which tells you everything you need to know about how the in-universe historians rate it: it is the start of a new era of knowledge production. Within 17 years it directly enables the discovery of the [[Fidon]] particle, the "second Higgs mechanism," and the "seventh time dimension."

## Effects / capabilities
- Enables rapid, theory-guided discovery of previously hidden physical phenomena.
- Arguably the technology that makes every subsequent discovery in the Timeline possible.

## Limitations / dangers
- Unstated. Presumably the downstream risks (voiding from [[Ribbondash]], etc.) are consequences of what the method reveals, not of the method itself.
