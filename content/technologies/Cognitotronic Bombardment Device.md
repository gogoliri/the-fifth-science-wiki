# Cognitotronic Bombardment Device

**Kind:** Machine: a desire-editing apparatus.
**Discovered/invented:** Built by [[Shervance Ek]] on [[Stara Lom]] over three years, shortly before 12,847 A.L.
**Based on:** [[Cognitophysics]] and the [[Cognitron]].

## Summary
Ek's formal name is the "high-amplitude cognitotronic bombardment device"; the narrator calls it **the Want Machine**. It makes "extremely fine-tuned adjustments to the delicate neuronal architecture of the brain", mainly to desires. The operator states a request ("remove his love of Nadastra"), the machine "understood the request", and with "a small flash, a puff of vapour, the smell of chlorinated swimming pool water" the desire is gone. It can implant desires as well as remove them. It is the first technology in the book that *edits* a consciousness rather than detecting, copying or transmitting it.

## Effects / capabilities
- Removes specific desires and dispositions: romantic love, longing, procrastination, reservations, care, doubt, striving, lust, ambition, goodwill, anger, hope, the longing for satisfaction.
- Implants desires, including self-propagating ones ("the desire to implant the desire in others to do the Good Work").
- Reversible: a subject can be returned to their default mental state.
- Operable by a layperson after a little familiarisation ([[Maximilian]]).

## Limitations / dangers
- Removing desire does not give contentment, only inertia. Subjects stop eating and stop caring for dependants.
- Removing the *reservation* about using it removes the brake. Implanting propagation desires makes it a contagion.
- Wiped out [[Stara Lom]]: see [[Stara Lom Extinction]].
