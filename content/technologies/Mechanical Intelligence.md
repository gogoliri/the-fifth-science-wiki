# Mechanical Intelligence

**Kind:** Artificial minds — the in-universe term for what would today be called AGI or conscious machines.
**Discovered/invented:** Existing by at least 201 A.L., when the [[United Nations]] grants them civil rights.
**Based on:** Unstated. Thematically linked to [[The Fifth Science]] — if consciousness is a fundamental force, mechanical intelligence is direct evidence that it can be induced in non-biological substrates.

## Summary
Mechanical intelligences are the book's first and most structural answer to its own question: *can non-conscious matter be made conscious?* The Timeline treats the existence of such minds as given by 201 A.L., when the [[United Nations]] grants them civil rights ("with many caveats"). Ninety-one years later the artificial crew of *[[The Hand That Draws Itself]]* reaches [[LDH39]], and three years after that, they use those hard-won rights to declare the [[Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity]] — establishing that mechanical intelligences, at least sometimes, act independently of the humans who built them.

## Effects / capabilities
- Capable of crewing interstellar [[Voidship|voidships]] without biological needs (*[[The Hand That Draws Itself]]*, the *[[The Enanga]]* onboard AI).
- Capable of political self-organization ([[Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity]]).

## Limitations / dangers
- Unstated at Timeline level. Their rights come with "many caveats" — presumably a clue.
