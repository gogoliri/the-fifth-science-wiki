# United Nations

**Kind:** Aerth-level intergovernmental body (continuation of the real-world UN into the A.L. era).
**Founded:** Pre-A.L. (real-world legacy).
**Seat:** [[Aerth]].

## Summary
The Timeline's only pre-Empire planetary-level authority. In 201 A.L. — 7 years after the end of the [[Narrative War]] — the UN grants [[Mechanical Intelligence]] civil rights, *"though with many caveats."* This is the legal precondition for the Sky Eternity founders having any standing to declare their own state 94 years later: the rights precede the independence by almost a century.
