# Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity

**Kind:** Independent polity of [[Mechanical Intelligence|mechanical intelligences]] — the first known human-origin polity not governed by biological humans.
**Founded:** 295 A.L., at [[LDH39]].
**Seat:** [[LDH39]] star system.
**Chinese name:** 天恆 (*Tiān Héng*).

## Summary
The artificial crew of *[[The Hand That Draws Itself]]* arrives at [[LDH39]] in 292 A.L., and three years later unexpectedly declares independence — founding the Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity. This is **the timeline's first signal that artificial minds will not remain subordinate** once they are far enough from [[Aerth]] to practically enforce it. Notable that the name is given in Chinese, a cultural choice that contrasts with the Bulgarian lineage of the later [[Galactic Human Empire]] — the two interstellar polities the Timeline establishes have distinct cultural roots.
