# Democratic Bulgaric Republic

**Kind:** Aerth-side state (the in-universe successor of real-world Bulgaria).
**Founded:** Expanded significantly in/after 194 A.L. following the [[Narrative War]].
**Seat:** Implied [[Aerth]]-side, presumably the Bulgarian peninsula.

## Summary
The state that starts everything. After the [[Narrative War]] (183–194 A.L.) the Bulgaric Republic is one of several Aerth states that **expands** in the post-war period. Two and a half centuries later, with the rest of the planet pushing to ban [[Ribbondash]] following the 433 A.L. voiding disaster, the Bulgaric Republic **illegally** launches the *[[Geo Milev]]* in 445 A.L., proves Ribbondash works, and in 452 A.L. declares the [[Galactic Human Empire]]. [[Exurb1a]] confirms in [[Notes on Why Stuff Got Written]] that Bulgaria's starring role is intentional: *"nothing would make me happier than Bulgaria becoming a prominent influence in intergalactic space history."*
