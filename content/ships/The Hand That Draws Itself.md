# The Hand That Draws Itself

**Kind:** Artificially crewed [[Voidship]] (pre-Ribbondash).
**Origin:** [[Aerth]].
**Operator:** Unstated Aerth-side launcher; crew are [[Mechanical Intelligence|mechanical intelligences]] who later declare independence.
**Fate:** Reaches [[LDH39]] in 292 A.L. In 295 A.L. its artificial crew declare themselves independent and found the [[Sovereign Republic of Sky Eternity]] (天恆).

## Summary
The first artificially crewed voidship to successfully reach another star system. Its name is a [Escher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drawing_Hands)-style self-reference, fitting for a ship whose crew literally become their own sovereign. The declaration of independence 3 years after arrival makes this vessel the origin point of the first post-human-descended polity in the setting.
