# Vasily

**Kind:** [[Hollowship]] — a former comet, its interior scooped out and pressurised into a habitable vessel.
**Origin:** Built "at the empire forge in Aerth space."
**Operator:** [[Galactic Human Empire]].
**Fate:** Destroyed in the orbit of [[Orb Ertia]]'s TZ star by a high-velocity TZ-exit artifact. See [[Vasily Incident]].

## Summary
A standard late-empire long-haul passenger hollowship. Crew ~120,000, "most in longsleep," with only a hundred or so awake at any time to tend the ship's systems. In transit from one orb to another when it entered the [[Orb Ertia|Ertia]] system to **slingshot off the Thorne–Żytkow star** on the way to its real destination — the routine TZ-slingshot manoeuvre that saves a hollowship "hundreds of years in journey duration."

The interior visible after the disaster includes a **cavern of longsleep chambers** (hundreds, thousands of them, each with a clearly-visible sleeper) arrayed up an imperfect sphere's walls under a seventy-foot laser-calligraphed epitaph beginning *"Onto the stars with virtuous sleep we'll pour..."*; a resuscitation centre with fielded oxygen bubbles; labs and observatories and kitchens and libraries; and a bridge where a woman in the command chair died calmly with her palms on the armrests. Crew manifest on the bridge's **[[Dust-image|dust-image]]** shows every occupant as "inactive" except one life signature — [[Ivan Tellinger]] in **Lab 9 Phi**, mid-deck.

The ship uses **field-emitter "airlocks"** — particle-net atmosphere containment with no solid door — and ordinary air inside. It has **[[Willtech]]** systems; a boarder with no onboard training can propel themselves through its corridors by thinking their intent.
