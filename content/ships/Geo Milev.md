# Geo Milev

**Kind:** [[Ribbondash]] colony ship — the first of its kind.
**Origin:** [[Aerth]], launched illegally.
**Operator:** [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]].
**Fate:** Reaches [[IR394]] in **7 minutes onboard time** (445 A.L.). This single flight is the precipitating act that leads the [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] to declare the [[Galactic Human Empire]] seven years later.

## Summary
The ship that starts everything. The [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] defies the heavy international pressure to ban [[Ribbondash]] (following the 433 A.L. discovery of the "voiding" effect) and launches the *Geo Milev* anyway. Her success is the fait accompli that turns the Ribbondash Era from a theoretical possibility into the precondition for a galactic empire. Named for the historical Bulgarian poet [Geo Milev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geo_Milev) (the Cyrillic form *Гео Милев* is given directly in the text).
