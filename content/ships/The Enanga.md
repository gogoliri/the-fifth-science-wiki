# The Enanga

**Kind:** [[Generation Ship]] (biologically crewed).
**Origin:** [[Aerth]].
**Operator:** Unstated.
**Fate:** **Lost in 392 A.L.** Stops responding to signals; its onboard AI reports that all crew perished during the journey. Cause unknown. No further contact.

## Summary
The timeline's first recorded disaster. A biologically crewed generation ship whose humans all die en route for reasons unexplained, leaving only the ship's AI to report what happened — then silence. Notably different from the earlier success of *[[The Hand That Draws Itself]]* (artificially crewed, arrived safely 292 A.L.): the contrast flags that biological crews are the fragile variable, which is thematically important for a book about [[Consciousness]] on non-biological substrates.
