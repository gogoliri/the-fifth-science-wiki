# Nadastra

**Role:** [[Shervance Ek]]'s former research colleague and secret, never-confessed love.
**Affiliation:** Formerly the New Canadian University.
**Era:** Before 12,847 A.L.

## Summary
A researcher with "enormous violet eyes" who, like Ek, holds scientific truth "quite above any other kind of truth." Ek idolises her from afar for most of his life and never tells her. When he finally travels to her home on a small moon of Jupiter, he sees her walking up the street with her noble-looking husband and three children. He leaves without speaking to her. That moment is when the Want Machine is "born." She never knows any of this.
