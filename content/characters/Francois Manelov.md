# Francois Manelov

**Role:** Mathematical physicist.
**Affiliation:** unstated (the surname is read as Bulgarian, consistent with the eventual [[Democratic Bulgaric Republic]] lead on [[Ribbondash]]).
**Era:** 419 A.L.

## Summary
Author of **The First Proof** — the first formal mathematical proof of [[Ribbondash]] physics, published in 419 A.L. His work turns [[Ming Shu]]'s 171-year-old topology casting paper into a physically realizable drive and sets up the first Ribbondash test in 431 A.L.
