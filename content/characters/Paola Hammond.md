# Paola Hammond

**Role:** Woman in her mid-thirties who sits alone reading in the poor-deck bar on the [[Sandansk]] waypoint station.
**Affiliation:** None stated.
**Era:** Empire era, unstated A.L. year.

## Summary
A bystander whom the [[Lantern]] names unprompted. It says she has "lived a fairly dull life, full of waiting. Waiting for the right relationship, the right career." She will be killed in a welding accident on the station's Construction Deck two and a half months from now. [[The Sandansk Station Worker|The narrator]] considers warning her, then recalls the paradox that trying to change her fate would only seal it. She is a mirror for the narrator's own life of waiting.

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]]: Her foretold death is the lantern's second demonstration of [[True Names|true-name]] knowledge.
