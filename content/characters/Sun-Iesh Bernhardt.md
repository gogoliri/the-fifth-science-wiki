# Sun-Iesh Bernhardt

**Role:** Wife of [[Isaac Bernhardt]]. Never appears in person.
**Affiliation:** [[Aerth]] / [[Galactic Human Empire]]. Citizen Number **2093/A9**.
**Era:** Originally contemporary with [[Isaac Bernhardt]]'s pre-experiment life; dead by the story's present — "long, long, dead. Our experiment here has outlived her by... three centuries perhaps."

## Summary
A memory [[Isaac Bernhardt]] clings to as he realises what is happening to him at [[Kaisure Station]]. He can see her face clearly, knows her citizen number, remembers the daughter who once played Miranda in a school play at twelve. When he breaks into the station's [[Hypergeometric Quiet Chamber|quiet chamber]] he addresses his plea for help to her at the empire hub — and receives a reply that reads like her voice, promising to contact high-empire on his behalf. The reply is in fact [[Oscar]], speaking in her voice: *"Darling, I haven't got time to listen to your complaining. Will you be back in time for dinner?"*

Whether Sun-Iesh ever knew what happened to her husband — whether the original Isaac even told her before he stepped into the caster's source chamber — is not stated.
