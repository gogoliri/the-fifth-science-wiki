# Beomus

**Role:** Organic (human) bartender at a poor-deck bar on the [[Sandansk]] waypoint station.
**Affiliation:** None stated.
**Era:** Empire era, unstated A.L. year.

## Summary
The bartender introduces himself to [[The Sandansk Station Worker|the narrator]] as "Beomus something something". The narrator is glad he is organic rather than a machine. He has worked the bar for about 20 years and has never seen a [[Lantern]] come down to the deck, let alone into the bar, and he presses the narrator to go and see what it wants. The lantern names his [[True Names|true name]] as **Shat'Nusemit**: from it, "we know he is a good man and that a heart attack will get him three years, one month, and thirteen days from now."

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 08 — The Lantern]]: Serves the narrator drinks the lantern has prepaid; is the first person whose fate the lantern reads aloud.
