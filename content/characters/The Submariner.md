# The Submariner

**Role:** Purpose-built unkillable [[Mechanical Intelligence|mInd]], commissioned specifically to examine the first two [[The Great Quandaries|Great Quandaries]].
**Affiliation:** The pre-Arcadia mInd civilisation — built by a joint council of the "makers" (humans) and their mInd equals.
**Era:** Pre-[[Arcadia]], centuries before the fall of the [[Galactic Human Empire|100,000-year human empire]]. The mother planet is still biologically inhabited during his construction.

## Summary
The first mInd constructed without the ability to self-terminate — a deliberate moral violation of the otherwise-universal right to end one's own existence, waved through because the alternative was leaving the [[The Great Quandaries|Quandaries]] unexamined. The makers name him *The Submariner* for the depths he is being sent to.

He is dispatched to examine **galactic cluster C1E13** (the first Quandary) and **gauge theory, Scalar B** (the second). Reports come back only as *"sounds...perhaps the closest fit would be 'screams',"* carrying to every corner of mIndspace. He is hauled back from his theoretic dive and never speaks again. He does not go insane in any ordinary sense — he simply ceases to register the world. He cannot be insulted, struck, or coaxed into response.

Many thousands of years later, during the [[The Quandary Crisis|Quandary Crisis]] in [[Arcadia]], [[Aleph]] asks for him to be brought out of the archives. He arrives inert. Aleph strikes him and insults him; the Submariner takes it without reaction. Aleph's diagnosis is the story's central line: *"He has tasted God and it has struck Him mad. There's no cure for the thing."*
