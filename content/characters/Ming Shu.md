# Ming Shu

**Role:** Scientist ([[Aerth]]).
**Affiliation:** unstated.
**Era:** 248 A.L.

## Summary
Publishes *Corollary Results in Topology Casting* in 248 A.L. The paper is not immediately applied, but it is later credited as laying the groundwork for [[Ribbondash]] technology — which does not receive a formal mathematical proof until [[Francois Manelov]]'s work 171 years later in 419 A.L.

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 02 — Timeline of The 500 Year Climb]]: Cited for the 248 A.L. paper.

## Related
- [[Topology Casting]] — the mathematical domain she extends.
- [[Ribbondash]] — the technology her work underwrites.
- [[Francois Manelov]] — whose First Proof turns her theory into physics.

## Open questions
- Does she live to see [[Ribbondash]] realized? The 171-year gap suggests not, but the story is silent.
