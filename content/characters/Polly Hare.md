# Polly Hare

**Role:** Mathematicist ([[Aerth]]); founder-figure of empire hypergeometry.
**Affiliation:** unstated.
**Era:** 0 A.L.

## Summary
Author of the paper *Towards an Effective Empirical Method via Algorithm Utilisation*, published in 0 A.L. — the paper whose publication defines the start of the A.L. (Anno Logicae) calendar. Her work is the root of the [[Hare Method]], which enables the discovery of the [[Fidon]] particle in 17 A.L. and every subsequent physics breakthrough in the 500 Year Climb.

By [[Ch. 05 — A Dictionary]] the compounding effect of her work is visible: the entire [[Causations]] field — and with it the empire's galaxy-spanning [[Hypergeometric Quiet Chamber|quiet chamber]] communications backbone — rests on what the empire now calls the **Hare decoherence process** for positrons. [[The Ertian Surgeon]]'s Empiral Archive lecture namechecks her directly while walking through the chamber's mechanism: the "decohered Hare positron" is the particle that, held across two causally-disconnected chambers, lets the empire talk across the galaxy without moving cause faster than light. Her name has become the adjective for a technique centuries after her death — the highest form of scientific immortality the book offers.

## Appearances
- [[Ch. 02 — Timeline of The 500 Year Climb]]: Named as author of the founding paper at 0 A.L.

Her devotees interpret this as support for the panpsychist tradition her work seeded.
