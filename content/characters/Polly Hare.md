# Polly Hare

**Role:** Mathematicist ([[Aerth]]).
**Affiliation:** unstated.
**Era:** 0 A.L.

## Summary
Author of the paper *Towards an Effective Empirical Method via Algorithm Utilisation*, published in 0 A.L. — the paper whose publication defines the start of the A.L. (Anno Logicae) calendar. Her work is the root of the [[Hare Method]], which enables the discovery of the [[Fidon]] particle in 17 A.L. and every subsequent physics breakthrough in the 500 Year Climb.
