# Jenny Dunne

**Role:** Old woman from the village of [[Wilthail]]. Fourth, brief [[Packet]] of [[The First Wanderer]].
**Affiliation:** None.
**Era:** Plausibly **~119 A.L.**, the Timeline's date for the first generation voidships launched from [[Aerth]].

## Summary
"A simple lady" who has travelled from her small village to watch "the first men and women to attempt to reach Earth's nearest alien star" — a rocket launch described as "all-white, elegant, a history torch." The passengers will be asleep for "many hundreds of years" en route, which pins this to the sublight generation-ship era rather than the later [[Ribbondash]] era.

[[The First Wanderer]] inhabits her only briefly, decides "this was early, too early," and exits. She is the narrator's first glimpse of the post-Earnest future and the story's clearest anchor point for dating the A.L. era: if the **119 A.L. first generation-voidship launch** from [[Ch. 02 — Timeline of The 500 Year Climb]] is this moment, then Jenny Dunne is our best pin for aligning Ch. 03's chronology to the Timeline.
