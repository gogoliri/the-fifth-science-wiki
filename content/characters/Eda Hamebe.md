# Eda Hamebe

**Role:** Retired empire causations mathematician — specialty "vaguely related to quiet chamber technology and causations." Great-great-niece of [[The Ertian Surgeon]].
**Affiliation:** Formerly the [[Galactic Human Empire|empire]] science division; now a private smallholder in [[Winchester]] on [[Aerth]].
**Era:** Late empire; many years after the [[Vasily Incident]].

## Summary
A woman "in her fifties," implant-free (rare on late-empire Aerth), living in an ancient stone house on a farm in Winchester with her husband — also implant-free — and their daughter Ansra. She speaks Galactic Standard perfectly; her husband's Anglish is his first language. Eda has left the science division and does not intend to return to mathematics. She chose family over the quiet-chamber work despite being qualified to work at the edge of [[Causations]] research.

She is also the named beneficiary of [[Tabitha Dimitrova]]'s old threat against [[The Ertian Surgeon]] — when the surgeon topolog's back to Aerth to visit her as his only known living family, the reader already knows the empire once considered her leverage against him. The threat never materialised.

Over dinner, Eda is the one who connects the dots for the surgeon. She tells him that the sphere that holed the *[[Vasily]]* contained a **[[The Dictionary|dictionary]]** — decoded from three-dimensional-binary holographic nanoparticles by a leaked [[New Rosance|Rosance]]-division paper — and that "some clever folk are using it to build new tech. Trying at least. Maybe one day they'll crack transmitting messages along the timeline and use it to send the dictionary itself back. A loop in a loop in a loop…" Her judgement is the line that closes the story: *"That dictionary is going to change things, really change things. Soon."*

She is also the one who gently tells him his first wife [[Henrietta]] remarried and died two centuries ago, and then offers to take him to the grave in the morning.
