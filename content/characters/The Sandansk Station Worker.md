# The Sandansk Station Worker

**Role:** Unnamed first-person narrator of [[The Lantern]]; a manual worker on a waypoint station who is offered recruitment as a [[Lantern]], and who is (by the ending's logic) that lantern's past self.
**Affiliation:** None stated; works long shifts, comes off them "covered in oil or glue or gunk."
**Era:** Empire era, unstated A.L. year.

## Summary
A tired, lonely worker on a waypoint station orbiting [[Sandansk]]. He has never recovered from the breakdown of his marriage and is waiting for a promotion to the Craftman's Deck. One sleepless night he meets a lantern in a poor-deck bar. The lantern tells him his future: the promotion comes in two and a half years and he regrets taking it, and he dies 30 years, 7 months, 4 days and 10 hours later in a decompression accident aboard a [[Voidskipper|voidskipper]] bound for [[Ithaca]], realising his true passions only as he dies. He declines the offer to become a lantern. The lantern then reveals that it is his future self, and that everyone refuses at first and reconsiders later.
