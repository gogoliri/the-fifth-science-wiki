# Maximilian

**Role:** Door-to-door missionary on [[Stara Lom]] who becomes the spreader of the [[Cognitotronic Bombardment Device|Want Machine]].
**Affiliation:** An unnamed religious ministry ("The Word").
**Era:** Shortly before 12,847 A.L.

## Summary
A "religious type" who knocks on [[Shervance Ek]]'s door with a holy book, wanting him to embrace "The Word." Ek removes his desire to convert anyone, then his desire to know truth, beauty and himself, and the two then remove lust, ambition, goodwill, anger, hope and even the longing for satisfaction from each other. After days of drooling stillness, one hidden desire from Maximilian's years of ministry surfaces: *the will to help others*. He learns to work the machine, brings in passers-by (a cyclist, then the mayor), strips them, and implants the desire to continue "the Good Work" and to pass it on. His leftover charity turns the device into a planetary plague.
