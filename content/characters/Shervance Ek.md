# Shervance Ek

**Role:** [[Cognitophysics|Cognitophysicist]]; inventor of the [[Cognitotronic Bombardment Device|Want Machine]]; patient zero of the [[Stara Lom Extinction]].
**Affiliation:** Formerly the New Canadian University.
**Era:** Before 12,847 A.L.; nearing 250 years old (middle age) when the story starts.

## Summary
A shy, polite physicist raised on the ice world [[New Canada]], who believes gratitude is second only to modesty. He spends his life in quiet love with his former research colleague [[Nadastra]] while living a "medium-happy" marriage (duets on the four-dimensional piano, equal chores). At ~250 he takes emotion-blockers, ends the marriage, and goes to find Nadastra, who is married with three children on a moon of Jupiter. Deciding the real problem is contentment, he moves to [[Stara Lom]] with his bonsai panda and piano and spends three years building the Want Machine. He uses it on himself, step by step, until nothing is left but the desire to remove desire, and then removes that too. He dies, along with the planet, without struggle.
